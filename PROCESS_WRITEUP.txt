UNION SQUARE — A LIVING SPACE
Process Write-up (draft — adjust after testing)

I chose Union Square because I pass through it often, but I realized that there is much more happening there than I normally notice. I visited at different times, walked around the park, explored the Greenmarket, and photographed people, vendors, open space, and the subway. One thing that stood out to me was how so many different activities happen in the same place. After getting feedback from classmates, I started thinking more about what brings people here and how the surrounding businesses, transportation, and public spaces are connected. I decided to make an interactive hand-drawn map instead of a traditional presentation or photo gallery. When someone moves their mouse over different areas, they discover my photographs and short observations. I also included a small “What brings you here?” feature and a brief look at Union Square's history. My goal is to let people explore the space for themselves and notice the everyday systems that make it feel alive.

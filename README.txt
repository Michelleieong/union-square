UNION SQUARE — A LIVING SPACE
Open index.html in Chrome/Safari (keep style.css, script.js, assets/ together).
Hover over the hand-drawn map to reveal Michelle's photos.
Click or tap hotspots to hold the card open; use card arrows for multiple photos.
Drag the map to pan; use buttons or scroll to zoom.
Left-top buttons provide activity navigation and a short historical aside.
Map is a schematic interpretation, not to scale. Historical and aerial reference images came from user-provided materials; check reuse rights and appropriate attribution before public hosting.
To submit: upload this whole folder to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages, or submit the ZIP if the instructor allows.

History timeline dates refer to documented site milestones; dates of the archival illustrations themselves are not independently verified. History101 reference: https://www.history101.nyc/places/union-square
